I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Rutgers University, in the Robot Learning Lab, advised by Abdeslam Boularias and Kostas Bekris. I train robot policies in simulation and deploy them on real hardware, including insertion with under 1 mm clearance on a robot arm and estimating hidden obstacles from joint torques and body motion on a Unitree Go1 quadruped. I also use learned models to make planning cheaper, for example by ranking push actions so a robot can clear a path through movable obstacles.

I expect to finish my PhD in May 2028. I'm seeking research internships for Summer 2027 in robot learning and manipulation.
